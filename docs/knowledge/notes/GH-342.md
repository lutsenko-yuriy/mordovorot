## Debrief summary

### 2026-11-18

**What went well**
- The brief settled the design one question at a time (wave order, fixed total time on the board, a short fixed gap in the preview, slide together with the headers gliding, interruption, Cancel waving back) and the ticket needed no revision during implementation.
- The plan's idea of an at-rest style equal to the end state made reduced motion free through the existing GH-162 catch-all, and the per-frame page rebuild stopped being a problem once each element got a negative `animation-delay`.
- Both audit rounds found real issues (an 8px dialog-height jump, a replayed shift slide) that tests could not have shown; the second round found only cosmetic ones.
- CI as the first compile was fine for the user: it caught a missing import and the a11y boundary rule before they saw anything.
- Videos recorded from a mock page on the real stylesheets let the user see the motion while away, and a quick headless height sampler confirmed the dialog height stays constant.

**What was hard or surprising**
- The container could not build, so everything compiled first on CI.
- Round 1's one decision (move the motion's logic into commonMain) sat at the bottom of a long message and was missed, so the round stalled until the user went back for it.
