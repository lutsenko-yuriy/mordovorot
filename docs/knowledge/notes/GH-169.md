# GH-169: Keyboard controls: one scheme for the terminal and the web

## Notes

- 2026-10-29: Left-handed keyboard play came up during the brief; parked as its own GH-150 sub-issue to scope with /brief.

## Debrief summary

### 2026-10-29

**What went well**
- The brief settled the whole design ticket in one dialog: the primer on shared-keyboard constraints (reserved keys, screen-reader single-key navigation, WCAG 2.1.4, board as one Tab stop) came before the scope questions and fed the decisions.
- One question at a time with a recommendation each time kept it moving; the scheme went straight into the ticket and ADR-0003.

**What was hard or surprising**
- "Digits always on" was agreed, then reopened two questions later when WCAG 2.1.4 came up; resolved as digits in keyboard mode only, with 6 (`[ Keyboard 6 ]`) the one key that works in mouse mode. The user saw this as what briefs are for: catching what may go south before development.
- The web mode switch went from a toggle, to "press 6 twice", back to a toggle once discoverability was weighed.

**What to change**
- Nothing.
