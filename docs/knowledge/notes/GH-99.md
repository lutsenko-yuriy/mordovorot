## Debrief summary

### 2026-10-17

**What went well**
- Everything works and the code reads well. The ticket turned out smaller than expected: one WU, two feature commits.
- The full workflow (brief, analyze, plan, draft-scenarios, review + audit) still felt worth it, even for a change this small.

**What was hard or surprising**
- Test seed: a 3x3 board one swap away from solved (`1 0 2 3 4 5 6 7 8`) is unsolvable (GH-74), so every load of it went to the `Can't be solved` dialog. Use an even permutation such as `1 2 0 3 4 5 6 7 8`, or a 4x4 board, which is always solvable.
- Keyboard Load dialog: moving focus onto a list row also selects it. Tabbing from the first row to `Load` passes over every later row and selects the last one. `SaveNamePrefillScenarioTest.loadRow` reaches `Load` without crossing a row: Tab from the last row, or BackTab twice (via `Cancel`) from the first.

**What to change**
- No workflow changes. The two points above are likely one-offs, kept here for whoever writes the next scenario test.
