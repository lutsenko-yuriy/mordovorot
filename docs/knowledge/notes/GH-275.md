# GH-275: Localizations: introduce new languages

## Notes

## Debrief summary

### 2026-11-17

**What went well**
- The result is outstanding, like the rest of the app.
- Splitting into small units (stubs, Russian, German, the mechanism, the picker) was better than the 2 units the owner first expected for the languages and the switch: each was small enough to review on its own.

**What was hard or surprising**
- Planned as 3 WUs, shipped as 5 (WU0 to WU4).
- The most surprising: after a mouse click on a language toggle, focus stayed on the toggle and the board's keys did nothing. Found by the owner on a desktop, after headless checks had passed.
- The toolbar didn't fit on one row in German or Russian (27 to 29 characters of buttons, room for about 21): found only when the picker was running, in WU4. It now wraps to two rows in those languages.

**What to change**
- Plan template: a test-strategy line for a new control outside the game's root (where focus goes after a click) and one for text changes in a fixed row (measure the widest words before choosing the wording).
- Follow-up for the owner: ask colleagues about the app's quality once the languages are out.
