**Amendment: no arrows on touch screens, as a new WU2 (after WU1 shipped)**
- **Old rule** (ticket and plan): no gameplay or UI change other than the footer; two production WUs, with WU2 (publishing) final.
- **New rule:** where the main input is touch (`pointer: coarse`), the arrows aren't shown and the board grows into their room; dragging is the only way to shift there. Touchscreen laptops keep the arrows. It is a new WU2; publishing becomes WU3, the final WU.
- **Why:** on a phone the arrows are small targets that take room from the tiles, and dragging is the natural touch input.
