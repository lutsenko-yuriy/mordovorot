# GH-189: Keyboard controls: a Controls dialog and a left-handed layout

## Notes

## Debrief summary

### 2026-10-29

**What went well**
- Another case of what briefs are for: generating ideas while it isn't too late. The user's idea of using the layout dialog to also show the controls, then of replacing `[ Keyboard 6 ]` with `[ Controls 6 ]`, came up mid-brief; since GH-169's scheme wasn't built yet, it cost an ADR-0003 amendment instead of rework in GH-170/GH-171.
- The primer (mirrored preset, extra bindings, full remapping, numpad) let the user name the option they wanted straight away.
- Parking left-handed play during GH-169's brief and scoping it right after kept GH-169 focused.

**What was hard or surprising**
- The Controls dialog reverses GH-169's one-key mode switch; the cost (6 → pick → OK) was named and accepted before the ticket was written.

**What to change**
- Nothing.
